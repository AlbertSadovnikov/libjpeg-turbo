# Webcam MJPEG decoding experiments

Start with [the investigation summary](../../mjpeg_speedup.md) and
[the optional embedded-frame Google Benchmark suite](../../benchmarks/README.md).

Current target: [fast MJPEG-to-NV12 for GPU upload and encoding](nv12/README.md),
including accurate/fast reconstruction, strided destinations and comparisons
with the optimized entropy decoder.

The latest [fused chroma prototype](nv12/fused-chroma/README.md) improves the
retained Clang PGO+ThinLTO NV12 decoder by about 5% with byte-exact accurate
output, or 5–8% with reduced chroma arithmetic and measured output deltas.
See also [compiler tuning](bmi-intrinsics/compiler-flags/README.md),
[PGO/LTO](bmi-intrinsics/pgo-lto/README.md), and the
[chroma latency budget](nv12/chroma-budget/README.md).
The [paired Y-block IDCT experiment](nv12/paired-idct/README.md) runs the two
luma transforms in separate AVX2 lanes and measures the fused NV12 path.

The [MCU unrolling experiment](nv12/mcu-specialization/README.md) compares
four-block expansion and guarded Y/Y/Cb/Cr entropy specialization under GCC and Clang.

The [AC lookup scheduling experiment](nv12/lut-pipeline/README.md) tests
overlap with coefficient stores: minimal metadata lookahead gives a small
accurate-luma improvement after fresh profiling, but no fast-luma gain.

The strongest entropy prototype is the scalar bulk reader combined with a
branchless 10-bit two-AC-symbol LUT and pre-shifted long-code thresholds:

- [Implementation, validation and measured results](multisymbol/README.md)
- [Self-contained patch against the original decoder](multisymbol/multisymbol-branchless.patch)
- [Original scalar bulk reader](entropy-opt/README.md)
- [Simulated and hardware cache investigation](cache-profile/README.md)

Patches are experimental alternatives, not an ordered series to apply together.
Production decoder sources are unchanged. The winning patch includes scalar
bulk refill, fused table cache support and the multi-symbol decoder.

Source, documentation and compact JSON evidence are versioned. Captured frame
sequences, executables, archives, detailed logs, disassembly, CSVs and Callgrind
profiles remain local or are regenerated by the runners. One camera frame is
versioned in `benchmarks/data` for standalone benchmark builds. Full-sequence
experiments require the original local `20261023T230418Z/frame_*.jpg` capture;
some runners also require the matching temporary builds explicitly documented
in each experiment. The scripts preserve the research workflow rather than
providing a portable production test target.
The fused kernel's assembly source and required headers are versioned.

For a small readable demonstration:

```sh
python3 test/webcam/huffman_walkthrough.py
python3 test/webcam/dump_huffman.py
```
